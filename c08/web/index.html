About this research prototype

NASA FIRMS observations over Pakistan. Detections are fire pixels on satellite passes, not individual fires. Spacecraft and orbits are illustrative. Seamline is an independent participant project; NASA does not endorse this application.

A finer detection grid can capture activity that a coarser grid misses. Overpass time, viewing geometry, clouds and smoke also affect what each satellite sees.

Pixel diagram is illustrative. Harmonisation compares overlapping monthly observations; it does not simply resize pixels or apply a universal multiplier.
